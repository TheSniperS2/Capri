# Atlas de paseo de Capri

Generado con la herramienta integrada de imágenes a partir de `assets/capri-personaje-v1.png`. Resultado: `assets/capri-caminar-v1.png`, ocho poses, cuatro columnas por dos filas, transparencia RGBA. Se conserva el atlas anterior para reposo, sentado, habla y parpadeo.

Prompt final:

> Create ONE production sprite atlas for the EXACT Capri character in the reference image. This is an animation extension, preserve her identity absolutely: enormous asymmetrical hot-red jagged lightning hair, one small geometric green face and single visible cyan/green eye, extremely thin long black angular body with red and green geometric panels, long pointed hands and legs, purple outlines, no teeth. TRUE transparent RGBA background outside the precise silhouette; no atmosphere, glow, gradients, shadows, scenery, labels or borders. The reference sheet has six poses; take its standing/walking character only, do NOT repeat seated/talking/blinking poses. Make a seamless eight-frame WALKING TO THE RIGHT cycle in EXACTLY FOUR equal columns and TWO equal rows, read left-to-right top then bottom. Eight sequential distinct phases: left foot contact, recoil/down, passing, push-off/up, right foot contact, recoil/down, passing, push-off/up. Animate the very long legs and arms naturally with small gradual differences across frames, trailing foot lifts clear of floor during passing, no sliding identical feet. Stable camera, same character scale, same hair shape, head and torso stay registered at almost same coordinates in every cell, planted feet all share identical baseline at 94 percent of cell height, adequate fully transparent padding so every hair spike, hand and toe fits inside each cell. No motion blur, no duplicates within one cell, no ghosting, no redesign, no chibi. Crisp cel-shaded geometric cyberpunk sprite art matching reference. A 4 by 2 grid, eight full-body sprites, fully transparent canvas.
