# Arte de despedida v11

Recurso: `assets/capri-despedida-expresiones-v7.png`, atlas de 3344 × 1882 píxeles. Cuatro expresiones de 1672 × 941, generadas por separado con la herramienta integrada imagegen y empaquetadas sin reducir su resolución. Orden: mirada arriba, mirada al espectador, boca abierta, sonrisa con ojos cerrados.

Referencias de identidad: [primer plano](https://www.reddit.com/r/TopCharacterDesigns/comments/1xf71il/capri_cyberpunk_edgerunners_2/), [cuerpo y silueta](https://www.reddit.com/r/TopCharacterDesigns/comments/1xbfssf/capri_cyberpunk_edgerunners_2/). Los fotogramas externos se consultaron como referencia y no se incluyen como recursos de la aplicación. El atlas v6 se usó como referencia de expresión y composición; un borrador intermedio corrigió la masa del pelo antes de crear las imágenes completas. Es una ilustración de la aplicación, no arte oficial.

Los prompts pidieron 3840 × 2160, pero la herramienta entregó 1672 × 941 por imagen. El atlas conserva ese tamaño real; no se presenta como 4K nativo.

## Prompts finales

### Retrato único

```text
Edit the supplied image atlas into ONE SINGLE FULL-CANVAS frame (NOT an atlas, NO panels, NO grid, NO duplicate characters). Use the upper right panel as the exact character identity, pose, crop and background. Wide 16:9 high-definition production anime closeup, requested maximum resolution 3840x2160. One Capri fills the screen. Broad continuous hot-pink angular fan of hair and a few long black lightning cuts, NOT many small spiky strands, same broad hair outline as supplied. Black angular face, flat bright green mask, perfectly circular blue ring irises with white disc pupils. Eyes looking at viewer, small affectionate thin white zigzag smile (mouth closed). Preserve the whole jaw, shoulders, black white pink/green geometric outfit and blue cloudy night background. Match the supplied upper right panel composition exactly, same modest head tilt. Crisp clean detailed cel animation linework, no blur, no grain, no camera damage, no text, no HUD, no watermark. Single full image only.
```

### Boca al hablar

```text
Precise local facial expression edit of this one full image. Preserve EXACTLY the image resolution, hair silhouette and all hair pixels, head position, angle, scale, black face polygon, green mask, both circular eyes and pupils, shoulders, lighting and night background. Change ONLY the small white zigzag smile into a small open white angular triangular mouth, like Capri saying a gentle goodbye. Mouth stays in the same location, width about equal to old zigzag, single clean white shape, no human lips or nose, no teeth detail. Everything else pixel-identical if possible. Clean crisp high-definition cel anime. SINGLE frame, no atlas, no panels, no text, no camera effects.
```

### Sonrisa final

```text
Precise local facial expression edit of this one full image for the final goodbye. Preserve EXACTLY image size, ALL hair and its broad pink silhouette, same pose and head angle, position and scale, black face polygon, green domino mask, shoulders and blue cloudy night background. Change ONLY both round eyes into two happy closed-eye crescents, black eye sockets with bright green upward-curving crescent eyelids, and make the small thin white zigzag smile slightly warmer with raised corners (mouth remains closed, like a content friendly goodbye). Do not move or redraw any other part. Same crisp high-definition cel anime linework. ONE full frame, NOT atlas, no grid, no duplicate character, no text or HUD, no grain or blur.
```

### Mirada a la estrella

```text
Precise tiny expression edit of the supplied single image. Preserve exactly its size and all pixels outside the blue irises and white pupils. SAME hair, SAME face outline, SAME green mask, black circular eye sockets, shoulders, night sky, composition, tilt, small closed white zigzag smile. Move only both blue circular irises and white disc pupils subtly upward in their existing sockets so Capri glances toward a star above her, while retaining perfectly circular blue rings. No other edits, no head movement, no perspective change. High definition clean cel anime. One full frame, no grid, no atlas, no text, no camera effects.
```
