## Prompt exacto

```text
Use case: identity-preserve edit. Asset: transparent PNG for Capri desktop companion, a new front-facing playful peek pose. Reference image defines the exact angular cyberpunk character identity: jagged vivid red hair, neon green face, black angular eye sockets, two blue circular cybernetic eyes with white central cores, dark jacket with violet trim. Preserve this identity and flat graphic sharp line style, not generic anime. Primary request: Capri is leaning over an invisible horizontal bottom edge with both clawlike hands resting along that edge, head upright and frontal, mischievous amused expression. Composition: complete hair/head and upper shoulders plus both hands visible, waist-up bust centered, balanced and nearly symmetrical eye placement, both eyes fully unobstructed and clearly separate. Eye cores should be clean white circles centered inside blue iris rings so the app can overlay moving pupils at runtime. Make a lively curious pose, looking straight at viewer. The hands align on one horizontal line near the bottom, ready to peek from a screen corner. Transparent background, no actual ledge/platform, no scene, no letters, no text, no noise or glitch; those are applied by code. Crisp edges, generous clear margin around hair and hands, landscape or square composition.
```
